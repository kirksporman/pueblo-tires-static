Oil change questions.

Short answers to what drivers ask us most.

How often should I change my oil?

Follow the interval in your owner's manual. Most vehicles fall between 3,000 and 10,000 miles depending on the oil type and how you drive.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome at every store. Booking online holds your spot and shortens the wait.

Can you change oil on my truck or diesel?

Yes. Our technicians service cars, trucks and SUVs. Diesel and specialty oils may be priced differently.

How long does it take?

Most oil changes finish in under an hour once your vehicle is in the bay.

Does this affect my warranty?

Regular oil changes at a certified shop help keep your manufacturer warranty in good standing. Keep your receipts.
